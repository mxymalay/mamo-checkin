# Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Effective date: 10 September 2026

## English

Mamo Check-in is maintained by the mxymalay project maintainer. It is an independent project, not an official Monash or Google product.

### Data and purposes

The extension processes your configured school email and Attendance name to verify the signed-in account. It reads relevant Gmail sender addresses, message subjects, timestamps, message text, attachments/images and source URLs, and relevant Monash Moodle course text, images and URLs to identify attendance codes. It reads course schedules and attendance status and enters matching codes into the school attendance website when a manual or enabled scheduled check runs.

Settings, course rules, message identifiers, progress/errors, codes and attendance records are stored locally in Chrome extension storage. The local OCR helper receives images and associated course/message metadata and records to recognize text and archive results on your computer. Local archives can include original images, source links, message metadata and attendance records.

The extension does not read or store passwords, session cookies or authentication tokens. Login takes place on Google and school websites. Existing browser login sessions are used to access those websites, and the extension may select the configured account in Google's account chooser.

### Sharing and security

The extension does not send your course or email data to a developer-operated server, advertising service, analytics service or third-party AI provider. It does not sell data or use it for advertising, credit decisions or unrelated purposes. It accesses Google/Gmail, Google-hosted message images, Monash Moodle and the Monash attendance website over HTTPS as needed for the requested functions. Submitting attendance sends the required form data to the school website. These providers operate under their own policies.

OCR communication is between the extension and a locally installed native helper. Exports and archives are ordinary local files, not encrypted by this application; protect them using your computer's security controls. Visiting GitHub support/download pages is subject to GitHub's policies. Information you voluntarily post in a public issue is public: never post credentials, live attendance codes or private course/email data there.

Use of user data is limited to providing the disclosed attendance functionality and user-requested support, consistent with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy and Limited Use requirements. The extension does not use the Gmail API or request Gmail OAuth scopes.

### Retention and control

Data remains locally until you delete it. Disabling scheduled checks stops scheduled processing but does not delete existing data. You can export your configuration and records. Clearing courses removes the associated extension configuration/records; the full reset action clears identity, courses, records and extension caches. Neither action deletes files already archived or exported on disk. Delete those files separately from the archive path shown in Settings and any export locations. Removing the extension does not remove the native helper or its disk archives. Standard browser, operating-system or third-party backups may retain copies under your own backup settings.

For privacy questions, contact the maintainer through https://github.com/mxymalay/mamo-checkin/issues without including private information. To request a private contact channel, ask for one without disclosing the underlying sensitive data. Policy changes will be published in this document with an updated effective date.

## 中文

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 10 日。马莫签到助手由 mxymalay 项目维护者维护，是独立项目，并非 Monash 或 Google 官方产品。

### 数据与用途

扩展处理学校邮箱和 Attendance 姓名以核对账号；读取相关 Gmail 发件人、主题、时间、正文、附件图片及来源网址，以及相关 Moodle 课程文字、图片和网址以识别签到码。手动运行或开启定时检查后，会读取课表与签到状态，并向学校签到网站填写匹配的签到码。

身份设置、课程规则、消息标识、运行进度与错误、签到码和记录保存在 Chrome 扩展本地存储。本机 OCR 程序接收图片、课程与消息元数据及记录，进行识别和归档。归档可能包含原图、来源链接、消息元数据和签到记录。

扩展不读取或保存密码、会话 Cookie 或认证令牌。登录在 Google 和学校网站完成，访问使用浏览器现有登录会话；扩展可能在 Google 账号选择器中选择配置的目标账号。

### 共享与安全

课程和邮件数据不会发送到开发者服务器、广告、统计服务或第三方 AI；不出售数据，不用于广告、信用判断或无关用途。扩展按功能需要通过 HTTPS 访问 Google/Gmail、Google 托管的邮件图片、Monash Moodle 和签到网站。提交签到时将必要表单数据发送到学校网站；这些服务适用其各自政策。

OCR 通信发生在扩展与本机程序之间。导出和归档为普通本地文件，本应用不对其加密，请使用系统安全措施保护。访问 GitHub 下载或支持页面适用 GitHub 政策；公开 Issue 中的信息任何人都可查看，请勿发布密码、有效签到码或私人邮件、课程信息。

用户数据仅用于已披露的签到功能和用户请求的支持，遵守 Chrome Web Store 用户数据政策及 Limited Use 要求。扩展不调用 Gmail API，也不申请 Gmail OAuth 权限。

### 保留与删除

数据在本地保留至用户删除。关闭定时检查不会删除数据。支持配置和记录导出；清空课程会移除扩展中的相关课程配置与记录，完整重置会清空身份、课程、记录和扩展缓存。这些操作均不会删除已归档或导出的磁盘文件，请到设置显示的归档路径和导出位置单独删除。卸载扩展不会移除本机识别程序或其归档。浏览器、系统或第三方备份可能按用户自己的备份设置保留副本。

隐私问题可通过 https://github.com/mxymalay/mamo-checkin/issues 联系维护者，请不要附带私人信息。如需私下沟通，请先请求私人联系渠道，不要公开敏感内容。政策变更将在本页发布并更新日期。
